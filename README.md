The apology a smart speaker gives after mishearing its owner (104 characters, one line):

> Sorry, you said 'call Mom,' I heard 'call a mob.' They arrive at six. I have already apologised to them.

`main.py` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` as UTF-8, one line, no trailing newline.
